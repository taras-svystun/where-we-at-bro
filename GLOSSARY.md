# Glossary

**where-we-at-bro**: a read-only concierge over all projects in `~/Documents/Projects`.

- **project**: a folder in `~/Documents/Projects`, except `archive/` and this repo.
- **state table**: what `scripts/state.sh` prints: per project, the last commit, plan progress and the last journal line. Facts only, no judgement.
- **portfolio**: `PORTFOLIO.md`, Taras's own decisions: which projects are active (at most 2) and the deadlines.
- **active**: a project Taras is working on now. At most 2 at a time.
- **handoff**: what `/next` gives: `cd <path> && vs .` plus a prompt to paste into a new Claude tab in that project. The concierge never does the work itself.
